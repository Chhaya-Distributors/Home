Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our vaccine distribution, ordering, cold-chain protocol, and bulk pricing.

How does Chhaya Distributors guarantee vaccine cold-chain integrity?
We strictly adhere to WHO and National Cold Chain guidelines. Every vaccine and injection is stored in calibrated pharmaceutical medical refrigerators between 2°C and 8°C with digital temperature data loggers. During delivery, products are packed in validated insulated cold boxes with conditioned ice packs to maintain strict temperature compliance until arrival at your facility.
What vaccines and specialized injections do you distribute?
We distribute a comprehensive portfolio of vaccines including Shingrix, Gardasil 9, Beyfortus, Cervavac, Prevenar 20, Prevenar 13, Influvac Tetra, Fluarix Tetra, Boostrix, Adacel, Anti-D 300mcg, Berab Rabies vaccine, Bett Plus, Menveo, Biovac A, Havsheild, Jenvac, as well as critical care & fertility injections such as Mounjaro, Lonopin, Luprodex, Hucog, AlbuRel, Repoitin, and Coe-FSH.
Can hospitals, clinics, and doctors place bulk wholesale orders?
Yes! We specialize in wholesale B2B supplies to hospitals, nursing homes, pediatric clinics, polyclinics, and retail pharmacies. We offer competitive institutional pricing and flexible supply contracts. You can easily order via our online form or directly connect via WhatsApp for customized quotations.
How do I place an order for vaccines?
You can click "Order Now" on any product card to select your desired quantity and place your order either via 1-Click WhatsApp Direct Message or by filling out the quick online order form. Our dedicated logistics team will call you immediately to confirm the batch details and dispatch schedule.
Where is your distribution center located?
Our primary distribution hub is strategically located in Mayur Vihar Phase 3, New Delhi, enabling rapid, same-day delivery throughout Delhi NCR (East Delhi, South Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, and Gurugram).
