About Chhaya Distributors

Our Story & Mission

Chhaya Distributors has been a premier cornerstone in the healthcare and vaccine distribution industry for more than 17 years. Founded with the mission to safeguard public health, we ensure that temperature-sensitive vaccines and critical injections reach hospitals, clinics, and patients in pristine condition without cold-chain compromise.

"Vaccines at the right time and at the right temperature save lives. We treat every delivery with utmost scientific care and clinical responsibility."

We understand that vaccine potency depends entirely on continuous temperature compliance. That is why our modern facility in Mayur Vihar Phase 3 is equipped with automated temperature logging, multi-stage refrigeration systems, and temperature-controlled insulated shipping boxes.

Owner & Founder • Chhaya Distributors

Our Quality Commitments

We maintain an unbroken chain of custody and rigorous quality assurance across all product lines:

  • 100% Guaranteed Cold Chain: Continuous storage between 2°C and 8°C with digital temperature monitoring.
  • Direct Manufacturer Sourcing: Partnered with GSK, MSD, Sanofi, Pfizer, Bharat Biotech, Serum Institute, and Abbott.
  • Same-Day & 24hr Express Delivery: Fast, insulated dispatch for critical pediatric, flu, HPV, and emergency vaccines.
  • Competitive Institutional Pricing: Direct wholesale access and transparent pricing tailored for clinics, hospitals, and nursing homes.
  • Emergency Prophylaxis Support: Rapid availability for post-exposure anti-rabies and tetanus immunoglobulins.
